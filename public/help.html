Help Center & FAQ

Welcome to the Na2slna Help Center. We're here to help you get the most out of your shared grocery lists.

How do I join a household?

You can join by entering the 6-character invite code shared by your family admin, or by scanning their QR code from the "Join Household" screen.

Is my data synced in real-time?

Yes! Every item you add or check off is instantly updated for everyone else in your household.
